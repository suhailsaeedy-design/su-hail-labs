WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES

One clear study workspace.

The project is presented by what people can accomplish with it, not by the programming languages or tools behind it.

DICT

Medical Dictionary

Search terms quickly, open clear explanations, follow related concepts and keep useful terms for later study.

3D

Interactive Anatomy

Move between body systems, inspect structures and connect anatomy concepts back to dictionary terms.

AI

Study Assistant

Use focused explain, compare, quiz, flashcard and summary workflows with clear educational boundaries.

OFF

Offline Learning

Prepared study packs keep important workflows useful when internet access is limited.

LANG

Multilingual Interface

The interface supports multiple languages and right-to-left layouts for users who need them.

ACC

Accessible Controls

Responsive navigation, readable layouts, keyboard support and reduced-motion preferences improve usability.
